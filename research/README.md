# Research artifacts

The CSoNet 2026 paper and its frozen NIDS drift experiment are under [`paper/`](paper/). For reproduction, use [`paper/experiments/drift_only/`](paper/experiments/drift_only/) and the manuscript/revision files in the same tree. This preserves the relative paths and script checksums recorded with the frozen results.

The [`experiments/`](experiments/) tree from `main` is also retained after synchronization. It contains the earlier split-layout copy of the experiment and quantitative results. Do not combine files from the two trees when reporting or reproducing paper numbers; use the manifests and checksums in the canonical paper tree.
